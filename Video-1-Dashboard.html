Speaking notes

The check therefore qualifies the AI-generated forecast. The model uses the management outlook to anchor its initial investment amount, but interpreting that amount as cash purchases still needs reconciliation with leases. Its later spending decline and 0.65 capacity-response factor are analyst judgments, not management guidance. Linking growth to additional investment is economically useful, but the particular relationship needs evidence. If more capital is required to deliver the same growth, less cash may remain for investors. The recommendation therefore needs testing against alternative investment paths, rather than relying on the source paragraph to justify the whole forecast.
