Segment revenue and margins drive profit. Investment and depreciation update PPE. Cash follows operating, investing, and financing flows; borrowing is an explicit assumed funding mechanism.

Limits and reproduction

Checks validate accounting consistency, not the investment thesis.

  • 10% WACC is a judgment assumption, not an estimated market cost of capital.
  • Segment margins and the investment/lease mapping require stronger calibration.
  • Terminal value is 94.2% of enterprise value; test the transition into stable growth.
  • The December balance sheet proxies the February date; no January–February cash-flow stub adjustment is made.

Another analyst starts with README.md, runs the model and tests, and compares the saved report. Fresh-environment reproduction and instructional-team repository access must be evidenced separately.
