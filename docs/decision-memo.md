**Business logic and recommendation-changing claim**

Growth must generate incremental operating cash returns that justify the investment required to deliver it. AWS growth contributes operating profit, while retail margins and working-capital behavior affect consolidated cash generation. Large infrastructure spending reduces near-term free cash flow. Faster growth is therefore paired with additional later capex in the model.

Management's approximately $200 billion 2026 company-wide capex outlook informs the initial investment amount. The later spending fade and 0.65 capacity-response coefficient are model judgments, not disclosed guidance. Whether this investment path is defensible is central to interpreting the recommendation.
