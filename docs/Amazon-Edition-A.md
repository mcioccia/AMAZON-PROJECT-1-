## Working thesis: test capital productivity

The research question is whether AWS and retail can generate sufficient incremental operating cash returns from the capital required to support their growth. The proposed distinction is between investment that creates additional profitable capacity and investment needed merely to sustain service quality, competitiveness, and existing demand.

The META note raised a related question about advertising monetization and AI investment. Amazon requires a different operating model: AWS capacity and pricing, North American and international retail economics, and shared infrastructure. The company change does not support a numerical before-and-after comparison of the two valuations.

## Valuation method and bridge policy

Use linked income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements to calculate FCFF, discount it at WACC, and reconcile enterprise value to equity value. Treat growth and reinvestment together, including a terminal reinvestment requirement tied to incremental ROIC.

The bridge must explicitly reconcile debt, finance leases, financing obligations, excess cash, securities, private investments, and dilution. Operating lease treatment must remain consistent with operating cash flows. Private investment carrying values are not automatically realizable cash. This note does not create a separate price target or present the existing model result as an earlier forecast.
